# Lead-location research, 2026-10-11: coverage log

Evidence file: `geocodes_2026-10-11.jsonl` (one line per lead: status, point, precision, source URL, date read, as-found excerpt, basis). Collected with `scripts/maps/collect_geocodes.py`; merged with `scripts/master/apply_geocodes.py`.

## Sources, cheapest first

1. **Own websites.** The contact crawler's cache (`runtime/contacts/http-cache`, read only) was scanned for Google Maps embeds and links, JSON-LD geo, map-widget attributes and plus codes on the sites of all three lists' leads. Home and contact pages of 166 unplaced leads' sites the cache lacked were then read (1.5 s per site, robots.txt honoured). A coordinate pair that several unrelated sites share (-3.3869, 36.683 for 'Arusha' and -3.3713, 36.8583 for 'Usa River') is a listing platform's default, not a pin, and was not used.
2. **OpenStreetMap.** Five Overpass queries over the lead map's box (named amenity, shop, office/craft/industrial, tourism/leisure/healthcare, named buildings): 5,344 named features. Name candidates were checked against the stored import distance where the record came from OpenStreetMap, and against the council's district boundary for ward points.
3. **Nominatim.** 49 street addresses; 7 results in Tanzania, none usable (all outside the map area or only a street).
4. **Official and reference sources.** The councils' captured website data (Arusha District Council's headquarters text and map point), Wikidata (SPARQL for the twelve unlocated wards: no coordinates for any of them) and Swahili Wikipedia (ward articles without coordinates). The NGO register's pins for the unplaced welfare organisations are shared or rounded defaults and were not used.
5. **Web search: 3 of 50 used.**
   - Q1/50 TCCIA Arusha regional office address: no address.
   - Q2/50 Canossa Dispensary, Arusha: no location.
   - Q3/50 Safari Plus Arusha office: only the Dar es Salaam head office.

## Not reached or blocked

- `tafcomtz.org` stalled the reader on its home page; skipped.
- Home pages not readable: 19 sites (DNS failures, HTTP 403, 500, 503, 522, a certificate error); `namiritours.co.tz` disallows its home page in robots.txt. Nothing was retried in a loop or worked around.
- Overpass: HTTP 504, 429 and 500 answers; the shop query completed on the private.coffee mirror.

## Held back

- KIWAKKUKI (welfare): its own contact page maps it in Moshi, but a precise point lifts its town-level hold and the welfare drafting step would then use a route the run marks risky (`no_risky_route_in_ready_drafts`). Recorded as `held_back` until that is fixed.
