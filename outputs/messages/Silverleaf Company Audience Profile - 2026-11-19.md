# Who we are writing to: the company audience

*As of 2026-11-19, from the master database. Every number below is counted from the database; the personas are working hypotheses to test, not findings.*

## In one minute

- We start from **1661 organisations** on file. 17 belong to the welfare or government runs or are excluded, and 110 are duplicate records, so we write to **1504 separate businesses** (1374 companies and 130 savings groups: 125 SACCOS and 5 VICOBA), plus 22 introducers (associations, chambers and HR bodies) and 8 savings-group introducers (NGO programmes and VICOBA support bodies).
- **1008 (73%) of the companies are ready for review now** (a draft and a usable route). The rest are held, mostly for want of a published route.
- The audience is **overwhelmingly tourism**: 709 (52%) are safari operators, travel agencies, hotels or lodges. The rest are banks and other companies, 221 microfinance lenders, hospitals, colleges and NGOs.
- **Founders and owners are the largest named role group in our contact list** (294 of 1308 people), and HR contacts are rare (31). So the person who decides is usually the founder, managing director or general manager, not an HR department.
- We ask **one of two things** of a company. A strong fit (it already funds education) is asked to sponsor students. Every other company is asked to set up a partnership on an education benefit for its staff's children. Neither first message states an offer.

## What they publish that gives us a reason to write

114 businesses publish a fact we have verified and quoted (the page and the exact words are on file): workforce 37, staff welfare 21, community programme 20, education programme 19, locality 13, other 2, growth 2. The rest get the plain partnership request, which is honest and needs no hook.

Of these, **14 companies already fund education** and are asked for sponsorship (variant A); the other 1360 companies are asked for a staff-benefit partnership (variant B); 130 savings groups are asked for a meeting with their committee.

## Introducers

22 associations, chambers and HR bodies are asked to share the staff benefit with their members (a bulletin, a members' or HR forum). One partnership can reach many employers' HR teams at once.

8 savings-group introducers (NGO programmes and VICOBA support bodies) are asked, once per wave, to introduce Silverleaf to the committees of the groups they work with. They are not companies and are counted apart.

## Personas (working hypotheses)

Use these to talk about the audience, and to decide what to test. None of this claims that any staff member is a parent.

### Owner-led safari operator or travel agency (494)

- **Who they are:** A founder or managing director who runs a team of office staff, guides and drivers, much of it seasonal and based locally.
- **What may matter to them:** A benefit that helps them keep a settled local team, at no cost to the company; for those that already fund education, a chance to extend what they do.
- **Who to write to:** Founder, owner, MD or GM. Often the only decision-maker; no HR department.

### Hotel, lodge or camp (215)

- **Who they are:** Larger resident teams in hospitality and operations, with a general manager and sometimes an HR officer.
- **What may matter to them:** Staff welfare and retention; a benefit that reaches many staff through one arrangement.
- **Who to write to:** General manager first; HR or the owner if one is published.

### Bank or other company (152)

- **Who they are:** Offices with an HR function and a corporate-benefits process.
- **What may matter to them:** A structured, low-effort benefit the HR team can offer; clarity on process.
- **Who to write to:** HR head or the managing director; regional offices often defer to head office.

### Microfinance lender (221)

- **Who they are:** A small licensed lender (Bank of Tanzania Tier 2 microfinance service provider) with a few loan officers, run by its owner; many publish only the owner's personal phone or free-mail inbox, which is not a route until confirmed.
- **What may matter to them:** A staff benefit at no cost to the company. Some lend for school fees, so later they may also introduce Silverleaf to borrowers (not asked yet).
- **Who to write to:** The owner or managing director, through the licensee's published phone.

### Hospital or clinic (188)

- **Who they are:** Large, shift-working teams on site, and a hospital director or administrator.
- **What may matter to them:** Staff welfare for people who work long and irregular hours.
- **Who to write to:** Hospital director, administrator or HR.

### College or university (23)

- **Who they are:** Academic and administrative staff, with a registrar, principal or vice-chancellor.
- **What may matter to them:** A staff benefit, kept clearly separate from student admissions.
- **Who to write to:** HR or the administration; the principal or vice-chancellor for sign-off.

### NGO or research body (62)

- **Who they are:** Programme teams, often partly funded by donors, with a country or executive director.
- **What may matter to them:** A modest staff benefit and a link to local education work.
- **Who to write to:** Country director or executive director.

### Public body (19)

- **Who they are:** Government agencies and parastatals, as employers.
- **What may matter to them:** An employee benefit offered through the HR route only, after a compliance check; never combined with a request for official action.
- **Who to write to:** The HR route; held until the compliance check.

### Savings group (SACCOS) (125)

- **Who they are:** A member-owned group governed by a committee. Members, not employees.
- **What may matter to them:** Information on schools for members' children, delivered through the committee; handled in a separate Kiswahili request.
- **Who to write to:** The committee; no individual published.

### Savings group (VICOBA) (5)

- **Who they are:** A community savings-and-loan group of about 15 to 30 members that meets weekly and shares out at the end of its cycle; many are women's groups.
- **What may matter to them:** A short Kiswahili talk at a weekly meeting on getting children ready for school, timed before the group's share-out.
- **Who to write to:** The committee, introduced by the NGO, bank branch or ward community development officer that works with the group.

## Limits to state when presenting

- Most companies publish only a shared inbox; a named person with a direct email is rare, and we do not infer one.
- The persona descriptions are hypotheses drawn from the kind of business and the roles on file. The first replies will tell us which ones hold.
- Reply counts will be small, so report them per variant with their denominators and read them as a direction.
