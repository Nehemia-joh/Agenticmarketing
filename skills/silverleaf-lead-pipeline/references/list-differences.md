# How the three lists differ, stage by stage

The company master is one canonical database changed one transaction at a time. The welfare and government runs are separate databases rebuilt whole from committed inputs by `run_pipeline.py --run-id <run-id> --rebuild-db`. Both runs open the master read-only, for campus points and existing IDs. Their verification fails if the master database or workbook changes during a run.

| Stage | Company master | Welfare run (`arusha-welfare-2026-09`) | Government run (`arusha-government-2026-09`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Segments and catchment | Employers by kind (`kind_of`), variant and reach tier; introducers (`Introducer: <kind>`); savings groups (SACCOS). Distance is a ranking factor only | Homes, family-based programmes, specialised centres, funders, welfare networks. A home beyond 25 km is held. Funders and networks are judged by whom they reach near the campuses | Councils, district and regional offices, wards and villages. Only offices covering the catchment; ward waves by distance band |
| 2 Capture | TATO and OSM (first list); gap census (`collect_gap_census.py`, `build_gap_census.py`); introducer research; user-named employers | NGO register (`fetch_ngo_register.py`), OSM welfare features, discovery agents (`plan_research.py`), gap census welfare records | Census wards, ward locations, OSM offices, council-site API, official document scan. **No web search** |
| 3 Validate and preflight | `validate_intake.py`, then `preflight_update.py` against the master | Validated inside the pipeline into `outputs/runs/<r>/intake-validation.json` | The same |
| 4 Merge and dedupe | One transaction per script (`add_gap_census_leads.py`, `add_introducers.py`, corrections, relations). Duplicates are kept and collapsed for sending | Exact identifiers and reviewed `links.json` (`nis_to_org`, `org_aliases`, `master_ids`); same-named twins get their own record keys | `links.json` `ward_aliases`, `osm_office_links`; unnamed villages are not imported |
| 5 Geolocate | `apply_geocodes.py` fills empty points; `--replace` with a reviewed corrections file replaces estimates | `apply_geocodes.py` writes `links.json` `geo_overrides`; the pipeline places the lead | `apply_geocodes.py` writes `ward_locations` (source `point`) and `office_locations` |
| 6 Distance and rank | `recompute_campus_distances.py`, `rank_employer_reach.py` (P1, P2, P3, X) | Priority (high, medium, low) scored in `build_welfare_run.py` | Ward score (0-100) and ward waves in `build_government_run.py` |
| 7 Plan research | `plan_contact_research.py` (P3 never searched; savings groups never) | `plan_research.py` for discovery; `plan_contact_research.py --db welfare` for contacts | Never planned for search |
| 8 Enrich | Crawl, OSM tags, direct-email extraction, Google Maps listings, call sheet | Crawl, OSM tags, direct-email extraction, welfare call sheet (`build_call_sheet.py --run-id`) | Council-site captures, document scan; `.go.tz` sites are never crawled |
| 9 Research contacts | Search agents, browser pass, calls | Research slices and contact waves; the register publishes no contact data; calls for homes without websites | Holders only from official rosters, official news (`holder_mentions`) and reviewed `post_holders_*.json`; verified only with two items, one within 120 days |
| 10 Merge research | Profiles, people screen, `merge_master_contacts.py`, `update_master_contacts.py` | `export_run_contact_research.py` writes `research_W_contact_profiles_<d>.jsonl` (supplementary: fills blanks only); `apply_research_reviews.py`; rebuild | `export_run_contact_research.py` writes `office_contacts_<d>.json`; rebuild |
| 11 Email formats | `email_formats.py`: candidates in `runtime/` only; the workbook shows the format only | The same: candidates in `runtime/` only, never in the run database | Does not apply |
| 12 Hooks and briefs | `plan_hook_research.py`, `apply_hook_research.py` (`lead_briefs`) | Education angle and funder relationship from research records | Convening signals tagged by the build |
| 13 Recipient | `resolve_master_recipients.py` with `recipient-overrides.json` and `organisation-relations.json` | Inside `draft_run_messages.py`, with `links.json` `recipient_exclusions` and `recipient_choices` | Addressed to the office holder by title |
| 14 Variant | `assign_message_variants.py` (A or B) | Funders get sponsorship; homes and programmes a partnership | Does not apply |
| 15 Draft | `draft_master_messages.py` (English, request first, cases A, B, C) | `draft_run_messages.py --track welfare` (English, cases A, B, C; routes outside Tanzania held) | `draft_run_messages.py --track government` (Kiswahili letter with the English meaning; a free talk and a parent booklet; no offer, never a benefit for officials) |
| 16 Track | `refresh_acquisition_metadata.py` (AQ00, AQ01, AQ02) | WA00, WA01 in the build | GA00, GA01 (core councils on official sites); GA02 needs a recorded introduction, so a research run assigns none |
| 17 Confidence | Companies and introducers: high-direct, high-indirect, medium, low | Welfare: the same bands; a free-mail inbox counts only when a reviewer confirmed it official | Every office in high, medium or low, with a delivery list per segment |
| 18 Verify | `verify_master.py` (9 checks) | `verify_welfare_run.py` (23 checks) | `verify_government_run.py` (30 checks) |

Merging run records into the master waits for Phase 0 of `plans/b2b-welfare-leads-plan.md`. Government records also wait for migration 0003 of `plans/b2b-government-leads-plan.md`.
