Three rules make the spine work for every segment:

  • S6 is optional. A partner whose win needs no signature (a circulation partner, a council introduction, a community meeting) goes from S5 straight to S7. The stage is recorded as skipped.
  • Agreement first. An employer that wants the agreement before any staff session goes S4 → S6 → S7. The staff session then becomes the launch event of S8, and is recorded as session held there. Each deal records which order it followed.
  • Nothing is dead. Silence is not a "no". A silent lead goes to Dormant after its planned fallout touch (section 5) and is re-tried once at the next intake window. Only a real refusal, an opt-out or a lead that cannot be served goes to SX.

How the conflicts are resolved:

  • The Process Map puts activation after the agreement. The spine allows both orders, and records which one each deal followed.
  • The Process Map's "Active" needs an agreement. The spine also counts a confirmed circulation as a win, because it is the partner's commitment and is in its control.

Side states and reason codes

Two side states sit outside the stages:

  • Deferred: the other side said "not now" and gave a date. The deal keeps its stage and gets a next-action date.
  • Dormant: silent after the planned fallout touch, or a partner with no new results for two intake windows. It is re-tried once at the next intake window (September for January primary, May for mid-year), or sooner through a warm route. After that retry it stays dormant, and reopens only on a new trigger: a reply, a referral, a newly named contact or an association introduction.

4. First touch: reach the right person

Approved by the user on 11 October 2026.

Why. We want relevance, not ambiguity. An ambiguous request sent to a general inbox (info@, admin@) is more likely to be ignored, and may never reach the person who can decide. So the first job is to reach the decision-maker for staff benefits: HR, the welfare officer, the COO, or whoever handles staff benefits and rewards.

The three cases

CaseBandFirst touch
A. A named person, with their own addressHigh, directThe meeting request goes straight to them, as now
B. A named person, but only a general inboxHigh, indirectThe full message goes to the general inbox, explicitly addressed to the person (the Attn format below)
C. No named person, only a general inboxMedium, or any route with no nameA short redirect request, not the pitch (below). A reply or a forward gives a name, and the lead moves to case B or A

Case B: the Attn format.

Subject: Attn: <Full Name>, <Role> – staff education benefit

For the attention of <Full Name>, <Role>. If this inbox does not reach them, we would be grateful if it could be forwarded.

Dear <Full Name>,

<the letter, written exactly as if to their own address>
  • The routing line sits at the very top, set apart from the letter by a blank line. It is worded to read naturally both to whoever opens the inbox and to the person once forwarded.
  • It uses the person's name, not "he" or "him", unless a source gives the pronoun.
  • No honorifics (Mr, Ms, Dr) unless a source states the title.
  • The MD or owner is named, but a large company's HR head is not. Address both. The routing line becomes: "For the attention of <Full Name>, <Role>, or your HR lead. If this inbox does not reach them, we would be grateful if it could be forwarded." The letter still opens "Dear <Full Name>,". Decision 21 sets what "large enough" means.

Case C: the redirect request. It has one line on what the message is about, the ask, and nothing more.

Subject: Staff education benefit – who should we write to?

Hello,

I am writing from Silverleaf Academy in Arusha about a staff education benefit for the children of <Organisation>'s employees.

Who looks after staff welfare and benefits (HR, the welfare officer or the COO)? We would like to send them a short note.

Thank you,
<Sender name>
<Sender role>, Silverleaf Academy

Any name or address the front desk gives, by reply, forward or phone, is recorded with that as its source and the date (for example "front-desk reply, 14 October 2026"). The lead then moves to case B, or case A if the address is the person's own.

6. The partnership offer and incentives

Approved by the user on 11 October 2026.

There are two different things here, and they must not be mixed.

  1. The partnership offer. These are the terms the offer register records: the staff school-fee benefit (OF04), the welfare partner rate (OF03), the member-association rate (OF07, once Finance extends it) and the family offer (OF01, OF02, OF09). They are stated plainly once the conversation is open: in the offer message (follow-up 1), in a reply, or in the meeting. They never appear in the first message.
  2. A promotional incentive. This is anything extra used to tip a decision, such as discounted first months or a waived fee beyond what the register records.
    • It never appears in any automated or standard flow, nor in a cold or early message (S1–S5), because it makes Silverleaf look desperate.
    • It may be used only at the proposal stage (S6), and only for a specific employer that has already engaged.
    • It needs Finance's written approval each time, is time-limited (with an expiry date), and uses only terms the offer register records. An incentive is entered in the register, with its approval and expiry, before any proposal states it.
    • The Rivertrees terms (OF08) are the model: negotiated after a meeting, quoted only to that employer.

9. Welfare organisations

Size: 445 in scope; 86 drafts are ready. Tracks: WA00 hold 321, WA01 routing 124, WA02 consultative 0 (it needs a licence confirmed by the authority). A welfare lead is an account that pays fees for the children in its care, not a channel to parents. These funnels build on F14 in the welfare plan §5.6.

Rules shared by every welfare funnel

  • Offer. The follow-up gives the partner rate (OF03), the free uniform (OF01) and the instalments (OF09). OF03 applies only when a home places all its primary-age children: 3% per child at 10 children, rising to 18% at 60. A smaller home pays standard fees with the family offer. Further institutional terms (VM13) stay blocked until Finance approves a terms memo.
  • Children's data. The lead databases never hold a child's name, age, photo or story. Admissions assesses each child in Ed-admin with the guardian's written consent. The databases record totals only: age bands discussed, places discussed, agreed and enrolled.
  • Safeguarding. Two staff on every visit, no photographs, and the DSL informed. A concern stops the deal, nothing is automated, and it is escalated to the DSL.
  • Recipient. The head of the home or programme on the ground first; for funders, the programme officer. A senior executive abroad is the escalation contact. A chair is used only when no executive is named.

16. Decisions

Approved by the user on 12 October 2026: each question takes the recommended answer below as the decision.

#QuestionDecision
1Who owns each stage?The Coord owns S1–S7 for employers, SACCOS, introducers and welfare, and the government letters. Adm owns every opt-in onward and co-presents sessions. The campus head hosts campus-based sessions. Fin confirms terms. The CEO signs agreements. The B2G owner is informed before any government letter. Welfare funders move to the Fundraising and Partnerships Associate once that role is filled.
2Minimum staff size for a site visit rather than a webinar?A site visit for 20 or more staff at one site within 25 km (or 10 or more within 10 km). A webinar for staff on several sites, on shifts or in the field. Under 10: a pamphlet pack plus a shared or cluster session. Use the count the employer gives at the meeting.
3Do variant A sponsors also get a staff session?Yes, but separately: the staff benefit is an optional second conversation one month after the sponsorship is agreed, never bundled into the ask.
4Community meeting format?15–20 minutes inside an existing ward, village or mtaa meeting, in Kiswahili, given by two Silverleaf staff (one from admissions), with the booklet and a voluntary paper form plus QR code. No photographs, gifts or payments. Inbound-only until the consented store is live.
5How are welfare-sponsored children enrolled?Admissions assesses each child in Ed-admin with the guardian institution's written consent. The databases hold totals only. OF03 applies only when all primary-age children are placed. A funder pays under a three-way agreement with the linked home. Reporting to funders needs the home's consent.
6Do introducers get a referral acknowledgement?Yes, non-monetary only: a thank-you letter and termly totals. There is no reward until the 2027 referral scheme is approved.
7Where do sign-ups live?In a restricted store outside the repository, owned by admissions. Run inbound-only until the data protection officer approves the notice and form, and PDPC registration is confirmed.
8Is the partnership closed at the circulation, or at the first sign-up?At the circulation or the signature, because those are in the partner's control. Sign-ups are S8 results.
9Session first, or agreement first?Session first by default. Agreement first when the employer asks, which is typical for banks, public bodies and international organisations. Record which order happened.
10Notice period for staff sessions?7 days with pre-approved assets; T-21 only for bespoke events.
11How is OF04 eligibility confirmed without a staff list?The parent declares the employer and role. HR confirms that one person's employment and grade, with the parent's consent.
12Who performs the public-body compliance check, and what fails it?The CEO or a named compliance lead. Exclude bodies that tax, regulate, inspect or license Silverleaf, and any body with official business pending.
13How small a number may a partner report show?Show "fewer than 3" for counts of 1 or 2.
14Use the escalation contact after a silent sequence?Yes: one message after the redirect-style close, then dormant. Record escalation contacts for P1 employers first.
15How are no-email rows worked (451 companies, 116 SACCOS, 233 welfare)?A front-desk call asking the redirect question: two attempts, then a hand-delivered letter for the nearest P1/P2 leads, then dormant. Start with the 50 hotels and 18 hospitals and clinics that publish a phone.
16Combine an employer-based SACCOS with its employer?Yes: one deal, with the committee joining at the session.
17How many sessions a week can the team run?At most three sessions or meetings a week until the Fundraising and Partnerships Associate is hired. Book P1 employers and introducers before the January primary window closes in December.
18What is the renewal and re-circulation cadence?Re-circulate before each intake window; review agreements 8 weeks before the January intake; a partner becomes dormant after two windows with no results.
19What may the government booklet say?Letters state no offer. The booklet may give the published levels, campuses, intake windows and the family offer (OF01, OF02, OF09) with the Finance condition, but no fee figures until the 2027 fees are approved.
20When may a promotional incentive (for example discounted first months) be used?Only at S6, for a specific employer that has already engaged, with Finance's written approval each time. It is time-limited, entered in the offer register first, and never in an automated or standard flow, nor in a cold or early message. Never for public bodies or government.
21When is a company "large enough" to add "or your HR lead" to the Attn line?When the employer is P1 or P2, publishes 50 or more staff, or is a bank, hospital, college, international organisation, manufacturer, aviation employer or public body, and no HR head is named. Small safari operators and guest houses are addressed to the named person only.
22Who may record a name the front desk gives?The Coord, recording the source ("front-desk reply" or "front-desk call") and the date. The name is used only in that organisation's Attn message, and is confirmed by the person's reply before it is treated as verified.
