## What changed

### 10 October 2026: enrichment and recipient rules

- **Government run.** The run now names the current District Commissioners (three changed in early October) and the new Moshi Municipal director, and segments every office into high, medium and low. The draft review fixed three errors: a ward 18 km away described as near, missing regional P.O. boxes, and a village office inside Arusha City. Six decisions on tone, form and the request (D1 to D6) came out of it and are still open (see below).
- **Companies.** 16 medium contacts moved to high through data fixes:
  - role ranking (vice-chancellor, rector, executive secretary);
  - repair of 32 TATO records whose fields were shifted by a column;
  - updates to contacts already in the master.

  A new own-domain rule (parent domain, sub-domain, or a same-name domain the company publishes) and email-first routing for verified MDs added more.
- **Welfare.** Medium fell from 17 to 9. The pipeline now applies `rejected-routes.json`; it had been ignoring it. Same-named funder and programme records now keep separate contacts.
- **Recipient rule (user decision).**
  - The first message goes to the most senior person running the Tanzania or Arusha operation.
  - A group, global or senior executive is kept as an **escalation contact** on the plan. That contact is not copied and gets no draft.
  - Board roles are never chosen, except a chair when the organisation names no executive.

  The escalation columns are `escalation_contact_id`, `escalation_recipient`, `escalation_route` and `escalation_trigger`, on `outreach_plans` in both the master and the welfare run.
- **High segment split (user decision).** Each track keeps `-high` with every high row and adds `-high-direct` (the person's own address or a matching role inbox) and `-high-indirect` (a general inbox addressed to the named person).

## How to resume

- **Read first.** Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, `skills/silverleaf-lead-pipeline/SKILL.md` and `skills/silverleaf-keep-outputs-current/SKILL.md`. The last one gives the rebuild order (steps 1 to 10) and the pre-commit checklist.
- **Databases and runtime files.** The databases and `runtime/` exist only in the main checkout. A worker in a worktree copies them in, never writes back, and reports both SHA-256 values. The orchestrator checks that the main checkout's hash is unchanged before copying back.
- **After any master change, re-run both run pipelines in the main checkout.** Their check `company_master_db_unchanged` compares against a baseline in `runtime/`, so a database copied back from a worktree fails it until the pipelines run again.
- **Google Maps rounds.** These are the coordinator's job, in the built-in browser:
  - Install the extractor from `scripts/maps/gmaps_extractor.js` into the tab's localStorage (`slfn`, `slsync`).
  - With the pane hidden, use `slsync` after a wait of about 6.5 seconds. Longer awaits can hit the tool's 45-second limit.
  - Export only the candidates' full listings and a compact top listing for the rejects. The full export of 266 leads is about 260 KB.
  - Build with `scripts/maps/build_gmaps_round.py`, then `ledger.py ingest`, `apply_geocodes.py` (preview, then `--apply`), `apply_gmaps_status.py --apply`, recompute, rank, and steps 1 to 10.
- **Search budget.** The cap is 200 web searches per session, shared by all subagents. On 10 October the workers used only 37, because searches about small Tanzanian organisations come back empty. Calls and browser reads pay off more than extra searches.
- **Commits.** Commit and push straight to `main`. Never commit `.sqlite` files, `runtime/`, `chats/` or `references/Offers & Discounts/`.
