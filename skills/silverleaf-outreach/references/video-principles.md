## 1. Connor Murray and Higher Levels

[10 Years of Expert Cold Email Advice in 36 Minutes (B2B Sales)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc), Tech Sales With Higher Levels, 9 June 2025. [Transcript mirror](https://www.withtranscript.ai/video/XLsAAnNaFOc). [Publisher's companion article](https://www.higherlevels.com/blog/cold-email-strategy-b2b-sales).

| Chapter | Distilled principle |
|---|---|
| [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=95s) | Avoid both long pitches and artificial personal familiarity. |
| [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=414s) | Prepare role/industry segments before writing; communicate identity, relevance and a direct request. |
| [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=764s) | Connor favours an initial email plus three follow-ups about 24–48 hours apart, referring back to the opening. |
| [19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=1146s) | Prepare responses to recurring objections. |
| [21:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=1308s) | Use a brief, professional three-part message. |
| [26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=1578s) | Separate broad segment work from deeper research on strategic accounts. |
| [30:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLsAAnNaFOc&t=1850s) | Examine opens, replies and meeting conversion separately. |

Chapter links identify topic boundaries, not exact quotation timestamps. His performance figures describe his experience, not controlled evidence for Silverleaf.

[I sent 10,000,000 cold emails and learned this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMndL5hNDbw), Clay, featuring Eric from Growth Engine X. | Time | Distilled principle |
|---|---|
| [3:40–6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMndL5hNDbw&t=220s) | Narrow targeting; investigate essential qualifying signals before optional enrichment. |
| [6:54–8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMndL5hNDbw&t=414s) | Test audiences and meaningful offers, not cosmetic wording. |
| [13:02–15:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMndL5hNDbw&t=782s) | Prefer three messages, approximately 3–5 days apart: initial relevance/offer/proof/request, added context, then a lower-friction angle. |
| [15:40–18:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMndL5hNDbw&t=940s) | Avoid needy breakup emails; vary benefits and explain the practical consequence. Reserve material for follow-ups. |
| [18:20–21:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMndL5hNDbw&t=1100s) | Attribute research, connect relevant case studies, and constrain AI to useful personalization rather than uncontrolled whole-email variation. |

## Decisions where the sources differ

The speakers disagree on cadence, whether follow-ups should mainly repeat or add value, and how much diagnostic weight to give opens and CTA phrasing. Do not claim consensus. Silverleaf's working choices are in SKILL.md and should be tested locally.

We do not import guilt-based pressure, invented account-team relationships, domain rotation to escape filtering, automatic quarterly re-contact, or the speakers' sending-volume targets. These are editorial choices for Silverleaf's relationship-based partnership work. Any future delivery infrastructure needs its own current assessment.
