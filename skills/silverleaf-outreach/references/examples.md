# Silverleaf drafting examples

Illustrations, not researched ready-to-send messages. Replace bracketed facts only after checking them. These examples adapt the framework to Silverleaf; they are not quotations from either video.

## Verified education programme, operations recipient

Subject: Staff education session at [organisation]

Hello [name],

I’m [sender] from Silverleaf Academy. Your [dated source] describes [specific education initiative]. I’d like to discuss a separate education information session for your own staff.

Silverleaf would answer admissions questions and handle interested parents directly. We would agree a session time with your operations team and check each family's campus and travel needs.

Can we arrange 15 minutes to discuss who should coordinate it?

## Uncertain decision-maker

Subject: Staff-welfare contact at [organisation]

Hello [name/team],

Who coordinates staff-welfare partnerships at [organisation]? I’m [sender] from Silverleaf Academy, proposing a short workplace session where interested staff can ask about schooling and fees.

Please point me to the right colleague so I can send them the outline.

## Insufficient evidence

Input: old daycare enquiry, no current need, no requester email, phone belongs to a provider's reply.

Outcome: `Needs research`. Preserve the historical enquiry and source link. Do not write to the provider as though they were the parent, assign an invented address, or describe the original request as current.

## Practical review

Reject “Your 225 employees need childcare” when the source described a 225-vehicle fleet. Reject “Our existing partnership” when only exploratory drafts exist. Reject “Ilboru is your nearest primary school” when the source assigns a centroid and Ilboru is a pre-primary campus. Rewrite the offer or leave a specific research gap.
