# Silverleaf campaign positioning and cadence

Version: 2026-09-09  
Status: Draft campaign system; no sending or scheduling is active  
Timezone: Africa/Nairobi

## Decision

Use the marketing documents to standardise the claims, tone, campaign gates and event timing. Keep recipient-specific hooks under the existing verified-only rule. An approved Silverleaf value proposition can support a message, but it is not evidence for a claim about the recipient.

Run public social publishing, direct broadcasts and one-to-one outreach as separate tracks. The event SOP supports frequent public Stories and posts. Direct prospects should receive one invitation; reminders become appropriate after an RSVP or explicit opt-in.

## Scope clarification for acquired leads

Organisation and business-contact leads use the separate [new-contact acquisition framework](new-lead-acquisition-framework.md). They do not inherit the marketing calendar, event cadence or parent broadcast plan. This document supplies approved Silverleaf positioning, claim gates, tone and value propositions where applicable. Recipient research and the acquisition track determine the reason, ask and cadence.

Existing and future contacts are assigned to AQ00 hold, AQ01 routing-first or AQ02 direct-recipient test. These are acquisition decisions, not marketing-calendar stages.

## Message house

### Prospective parents

Lead with a useful next step: help the parent identify the right campus and current admissions information from the student's age or grade and travel area. Support this with durable public value pillars: English-medium teaching, digital learning, student wellness and co-curricular enrichment. Use Kiswahili for direct parent communication, with a native-language review before release; use English for public online communication.

### Employer and institutional partners

Lead with the staff benefit and the low lift for the organisation: Silverleaf Academy can provide one optional Q&A and a concise guide; the organisation decides whether to circulate the invitation; interested parents contact Silverleaf directly. Do not assert that staff are parents or need school places. The recipient's role decides the ask: review the outline, nominate the right owner or suggest a workable format.

### SACCOS

Offer the family savings every Silverleaf family can use (the free uniform for full-year payment, the sibling discount and four instalments) and the member-association group rate, exactly as recorded in the [offer register](offer-register.md). Do not imply a loan, payroll deduction or subsidy. Finance must agree to extend the group rate, which is documented for KINEFA, and must confirm the terms for 2027 before sending.

### Existing Silverleaf families at early-years campuses

Treat progression to primary as a separate warm journey. Explain the transition options early, offer a visit and record the decision. This is a design recommendation from the business analysis and requires a current family roster, confirmed campus offerings and a responsible admissions owner before activation.

## Claims held out of evergreen campaign copy

- Fee figures for 2027 until the fee structure is approved. The current site and 2026 brochure publish figures, but the 2027 Gantt chart shows approval work in progress.
- Discounts and partner terms other than those in the [offer register](offer-register.md). Register terms may appear in drafts, each with the condition that Finance confirms them for 2027 before sending.
- Referral rewards, until the 2027 referral scheme is approved (2027 Gantt task A8).
- Student counts, occupancy, retention, exam, social-emotional-learning or teacher-training statistics until the metric source of truth is reconciled.
- Campus capacity, available places, transport coverage or a visit slot until admissions confirms it.
- “Leading”, “largest”, “world-class” or outcome language when the message does not need it. Prefer concrete value pillars.
- Any hook about a recipient, organisation, workforce or community unless its exact claim has a current, traceable source.

## Tone and language controls

- First mention: “Silverleaf Academy”; later mentions may use “Silverleaf”.
- Use simple, friendly, conversational wording and UK English.
- Use “student”, “our students”, “our school” and “high-quality”.
- Avoid “SLA”, “the academy”, “our kids”, “our children” and the standalone adjective “quality”.
- Use Kiswahili for direct parent print, digital and face-to-face communication; use English for public website and social media. Where the source inquiry was in English, reply in English unless the person indicates another preference.
- Keep one answerable call to action per touchpoint.

## Cadence decisions

### Personalised partnership outreach

Keep the current proposed test of Day 0, four working days, then four working days. The marketing documents do not prescribe a B2B follow-up interval, so this remains a test setting derived from the earlier outreach principles. Stop after three delivered messages. Stop earlier on any reply, referral, refusal, opt-out, hard bounce or out-of-office response.

### Fresh prospective-parent enquiry

Respond in the same working period where operationally possible and no later than the next working day. Ask only for age or grade and travel area. If there is no reply, send one helpful reminder after two working days and a close-the-loop message five working days later. This is a proposed service-level test; measure response and adjust. Stop on reply, application, visit booking, refusal or opt-out.

### Historical public enquiry

One contextual requalification reply only when the original author, date, request and reply route are clear and the platform still invites a response. No automatic follow-up.

### Event and open-day communication

Use the SOP as the internal production clock: confirm the brief and audience at T-21 days, complete review by T-14, and approve every asset at least 48 hours before its scheduled post. The 2026 open-day calendar commonly says “pre-promote two weeks prior”, while the SOP begins event activity three weeks out. Reconcile this by starting internal work at T-21 and using T-14 as the default external invitation unless the approved event brief specifies an earlier announcement.

For direct leads, send one T-14 invitation. Only RSVP or opted-in recipients receive T-2 logistics and a T-1 final reminder. Send a thank-you or next-step message within 24 hours after the event. Public social channels may follow the fuller SOP cadence.

### 2027 admissions broadcasts

Treat the September, October, November and December entries in the Gantt chart and calendar as a conditional plan. Each is a separate approved broadcast to an opted-in or appropriate audience. Suppress later broadcasts after an application, enrolment, refusal or opt-out. Replace urgency language with a real date or remove it.

Suggested plan from the internal schedule:

| Candidate date | Purpose | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-25 | 2027 admissions announcement and interest capture | Confirm 2027 facts, application route and finished collateral |
| 2026-10-31 | Primary-intake launch and campus-fit prompt | Confirm campaign launch and any linked open day |
| 2026-11-30 | Practical admissions help and visit prompt | Confirm capacity and actual deadline; no false “last chance” |
| 2026-12-31 | New-year check-in for undecided opt-ins | Confirm office coverage and response owner |

### Feeder-campus progression

Proposed test: T-12 weeks explain the upcoming primary transition; T-8 weeks share a confirmed primary-campus guide; T-4 weeks offer a visit; T-1 week close the loop with a human review. This flow is dormant until a current family roster, consent basis, campus offerings and dates are loaded.

### Referral programme

Keep referral automation blocked. The 2027 Gantt chart schedules referral-scheme review and approval through 2026-10-30. Do not draft rewards, incentives or referral terms until approval is recorded.

## Measurement and learning

Track unique delivered accounts, positive replies, referrals, meetings, sessions, parent opt-ins, tours, applications and enrolments. For parent campaigns, track age/grade supplied, locality supplied, campus match, visit offered, visit booked and visit attended as separate events. For events, track registration, attendance and post-event admissions action separately from social reach.

Record the source and variant for every result. After each campaign, write what was sent, what the audience responded to and what should change. Do not treat opens, impressions or a calendar entry as an enrolment outcome.
