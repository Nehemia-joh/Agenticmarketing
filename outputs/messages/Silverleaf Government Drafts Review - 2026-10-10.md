# Government drafts review, 2026-10-10

Review of `Silverleaf Government Drafts - 2026-10-01.md` (149 letters: 9 councils, 7 district and 3 regional offices, 128 wards and
2 villages), generated by `scripts/messaging/draft_run_messages.py` and `consolidate_government_drafts.py`. Checked against the run
database, the official sources behind it (council and regional sites captured again on 10 October 2026), the angle stated in the
drafts file, and Tanzanian letter protocol.

Corrected letters: `Silverleaf Government Drafts - 2026-10-10.md`. Segments: `Silverleaf Government Segments - 2026-10-10.md`.

Severity: **High** = the letter would go to the wrong person or office, or state something false; **Medium** = a gap that a reader
would notice, or a claim that rests on an estimate; **Low** = form and polish.

## Summary

- **The addresses and titles of the four pilot council letters are right.** P.O. boxes match the councils' own documents (Arusha City
  3013 Arusha, Arusha District 2330 Arusha, Hai 27 Hai, Meru 462 Usa River), and each goes to the right office.
- **No letter breaks the angle or the rules.** None states an offer, a fee or a service. None offers officials anything, takes a side
  in politics or criticises public schools. The order is right: the council introduction first, the wards only after it.
- **The office holders changed under the drafts.** Between 23 September and 10 October 2026:
  - Moshi Municipal Council has a new director.
  - The Arusha, Arumeru and Hai District Commissioners were replaced, on 1 to 3 October.

  The letters are addressed by title, so their wording still holds. But the names on file for the cover note, the call and the visit
  were out of date. They are corrected in the data.
- **Three factual corrections are applied through the pipeline:**
  - Hai's letter named a ward 18 km away as "near our campuses".
  - The regional letters had no postal address.
  - A "village" office inside Arusha City is now flagged.
- **Six decisions are left for you.** They touch tone, form or the request, so they are not applied. Each has a recommended option.
